Entry Step Branch User choice System message Design note
  1. The wake word is the whole product. A button would mean picking up a tablet, unlocking it and finding a form — which is the 8pm-from-memory behaviour this exists to replace. Detecting it on-device is also the answer to the first question every provider asks about a live microphone in a room full of other people's children.
  2. A meaningful share of providers in this market work primarily in Spanish. Translating ahead of the matchers rather than running a second parser means one phrase library to maintain, and no drift between the two languages.
  3. The parse path is deterministic on purpose. A hallucinated category writes a nap that never happened into a record a parent reads and a licensor may audit — so the rules engine either matches or does not, and what it does not match goes to a human. The language model sits ahead of this instead: when a child is added, it pre-computes the ways their name is likely to be misheard.
  4. Recognition will miss, so a miss is modelled rather than mourned. The event is still written, carrying the two diagnostic flags, the raw utterance and the audio — which is what makes repair take seconds instead of re-remembering the moment.
  5. Correcting a name does not just fix one record. The tapped word is appended to that child's alias list, so repair work compounds — the vocabulary improves from human correction and from the model's pre-computation at the same time.
